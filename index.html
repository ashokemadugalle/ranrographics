About Ashoke Salon

Founded in 2010, Ashoke Salon has been a beacon of beauty and relaxation in our community. Our founder, Mrs. Ashoke Perera, envisioned a space where clients could experience luxury treatments at affordable prices.

Our team consists of highly trained professionals who regularly update their skills with the latest techniques and trends in the beauty industry.

We pride ourselves on using only premium, cruelty-free products that deliver exceptional results while being gentle on your skin and hair.
